# PDL Pitch Deck Design

**Workstream: deck | Draft for review | 2026-07-02**
This is PDL's internal working plan — the strategy we (the team) are building, presented for alignment. It is not a vendor pitch. Primary use: team alignment (ratify category, sacrifice tier, and roadmap). It's ~80% reusable as an external raise/partner deck later by swapping the noted slides.

---

## Design direction

- **Visual metaphor:** the black box vs. the glass box. Dark, opaque panels for the enemy/problem slides; the deck literally lightens as the story moves to the PDL model. One recurring device: a "what you can see" annotation layer that appears on PDL-side diagrams.
- **Evidence styling:** every stat gets a small source chip (r/hwstartups, Cadstrom, Instrumental, etc.) - the deck practices the transparency it sells.
- **Typography/feel:** engineering-document honesty (grid, mono accents for numbers) over agency gloss. The anti-render aesthetic.
- **Rule:** world before product. No PDL logo-slide chest-beating until slide 8.

## Slide-by-slide

**1. Title.** "Nothing behind the curtain." PDL wordmark, date, one line: *The plan for Product Design Labs, built on 30 days of market evidence.*

**2. The Shift.** Hardware is back. Three proof chips: hardware seeds run $5-10M (2-3x software) / June closes: Etched $800M, Stathera $55M / "AI can't do manufacturing, supply chains, or physics." Visual: funding momentum arrows.

**3. The Stakes.** The minefield. Big stat wall: 90% of first prototypes miss / 2.9 respins avg, ~$70K true cost each / founders plan in months, honest timelines run years (the 6-year wearable). Visual: dark panel, stats punched through it.

**4. The Enemy (named).** Black-box product development. The four signatures as icons: the render trap, withheld files, lowest-bid 2-3x rework, licensed-not-assigned IP. Pull quote from the burn research ("$80K to a front company... lawsuit"). This slide is the emotional core - keep it stark.

**5. The Promised Land.** What founders actually want: know what exists every week, own every file already, show investors mid-stream without flinching. Visual: first light slide - the glass box appears.

**6. The Category Insight.** "The fix isn't a better agency. It's development where openness is the operating system." Introduce the category name - **layout holds both options**: Option A "Open Product Development" (Jason's pick) and Option B "Glass-Box Product Development," with the Part-2 decision table as a builder note. **This is the slide Reggie's team ratifies.**

**7. The Model.** PDL's four commitments as one diagram: DFM day one / yours at every milestone / honest stage gates (EVT 4-5wk, DVT 8wk, PVT 4wk floors) / math in writing (respins budgeted in the SOW). Each commitment mapped to the burn it eliminates.

**8. Who we are.** PDL: hybrid studio + services - and the arc, stated openly: services fund the studio; as PDL products gain footholds, the mix shifts toward our own products. Visual: timeline with the transition marker as an open question for the team.

**9. The proof plan.** Honest slide: proof is the current gap, here's how it fills - live devlog before site launch, published artifacts (deliverable manifest, SOW respin clause, calculator), first case studies. (External raise version: replace with our existing portfolio &amp; traction.)

**10. The content engine.** The four pillars as a flywheel: Open Devlog → Burn Files → Honest Math (calculator = lead magnet) → Funding Ladder → inbound services + studio deal flow. Chip: every format validated by 30-day engagement evidence.

**11. The product bets.** Thesis backlog as a portfolio grid with probes: Relic (scan-to-print, $99 probe, 2wk) / BOMcheck (calculator paid tier, ships free anyway) / Micro-factory (brokered runs, no capex) / Open flagship (held). Kill-criteria printed on the slide - the anti-"noise-to-signal" studio discipline, visible.

**12. Funding ops.** The ladder graphic: SBIR I ($200-305K) → SBIR II ($2M) → accelerators ($550K) → pre-seed → $5-10M hardware seed. Callout: SBIR reauthorized to 2031, new rules favor first-timers, non-dilutive. PDL capability: funding-path memo on every project + SBIR pilot.

**13. The sacrifice (team decision slide).** The three openness tiers side by side, with what each unlocks and what each costs operationally. Recommended floor: Tier 2. **This is the Reggie conversation, on one slide.** (Cut from investor version.)

**14. The 90 days.** Week-banded roadmap: checklist + Devlog #1 (wk 1-2) → calculator + respin essay (wk 3-6) → r/hwstartups retrospective + funding ladder (wk 7-10) → first Burn File + probe reviews (wk 11-13). Owners column left blank for the meeting.

**15. Close.** The one-liner: *Hardware development you can actually see.* Three asks for the room: ratify the category name / pick the sacrifice tier / assign the 90-day owners.
