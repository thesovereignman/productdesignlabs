| Field | Line |
|-------|------|
| **LEAD** | A bootstrap hardware team (PDL × Mohyi Labs) racing a thirty-day clock. |
| **WANT** | Competition-ready heavy-lift drone under a brutal weight limit — proven in a serious room. |
| **FORCE AGAINST** | Tariffs. Print failures. Missing parts. Physics. |
| **TURN** | Iterate on the bench. Print what lead times won’t allow. Show struggle; protect recipe. |
