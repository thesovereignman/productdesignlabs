## 2. Story structure kit (same 5 energies everywhere)

Used for Mohyi flagship + both Trinion lanes:

1. **Clock drama** (inverted pyramid) — start at stakes/room/outcome → rewind  
2. **Constraint as antagonist** — name the force against; fight it  
3. **Second-person immersion** — “you” unity start to finish  
4. **Quiet judgment** — antithesis essay (deck vs bench, chemicals vs dry, etc.)  
5. **Dispatch / field note** — dated beats, reporter cadence

- Eloquence tools: antithesis, anaphora, tricolon, asyndeton, rhetorical question — lightly. Avoid unverified hyperbole.  
- Sophisticated storytellers — never nursery framing.
