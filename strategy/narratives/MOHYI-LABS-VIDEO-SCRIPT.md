## 2. Longer cut VO (90–150s)

> **Flagship choice:** For the 45–75s Reel pick, use one of the five variants in §1. This longer cut stays as extended / YouTube / LinkedIn native VO.

**Working title:** The Mohyi Labs Sprint  
**Use:** YouTube extended / IG carousel VO / LinkedIn native

**VO script (Reggie voice — direct, struggle-forward):**

We’re not warming up. We’re in it.

Mohyi Labs. A competition drone with a hard weight cap and a payload that shouldn’t fit the math — out and back a few nautical miles, takeoff and landing that have to be clean. The room in Ohio isn’t a feed. It’s government, VCs, investors, every branch of the military watching whether the thing is real.

We got pulled in on a short fuse. Amir’s cousin John had the competition path. We said yes. The clock started.

Power scheme. Off-the-shelf electronics where we could. 3D-printed structure because carbon fiber lead times don’t care about your calendar. New impeller design that works — and weighs more than we want. Hail Mary calls to shops that can print nylon big enough. Tariff chaos. Drone parts that used to come from China that you can’t just order anymore. So you buy late. You iterate. We’ve been through something like thirty designs to get here.

Matt’s been modeling night and day. I burned myself on a hot drill bit assembling. The printers fail. We start again. We’re time-lapsing the whole house shop on a 360 so nobody can call this vaporware.

End of this week: prototype up, then firmware. Next: Chicago for final assembly. Then Ohio.

If we pull this off, it won’t be because the deck was pretty. It’ll be because we bullied the constraints until the machine existed.

Follow the Build. Mohyi Labs. Product Design Labs.
